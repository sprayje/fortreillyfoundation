OUR WHY

Built on sacrifice.
Driven by service.

The Fort Reilly Foundation exists to honor the memory of Lance Corporal TJ Reilly, who made the ultimate sacrifice on December 21, 2008, in Karmah, Iraq. His courage and selflessness continue to inspire everything we do.

We support veterans and military families across Kentucky through direct assistance, community events, resources, and by keeping the stories of our fallen heroes alive.
